A second opinion that actually pushes back.

Claude and ChatGPT are better at catching each other's mistakes than their own. spar does the copy-paste between them, and keeps them honest.

  1. Serve

    You ask anything: a decision, a plan, a draft, a bug. One side gives its best answer, searching the web if it needs to.

  2. Return

    The other side looks for what's wrong, missing or invented, and sends back a better version. You can add a note on any turn.

  3. Concede

    It ends when one side looks at the other's answer and can't find a hole, or when you say so.

The desktop app, for work you'll come back to.

Questions

Does it use my Claude and ChatGPT accounts?
Yes. spar runs the official Claude Code and Codex tools on your computer, signed in by you. It never sees your password or tokens, and your prompts go only to Anthropic and OpenAI.
Won't they just agree with each other?
Both get the same short rules: be right, not agreeable; say pass instead of making busywork changes; mark anything uncertain; never invent sources.
Is it really free?
The command-line version is free and open source under Apache 2.0, for good. The desktop app has a free plan and a Pro plan.

# Privacy

The spar command-line tool collects nothing.

- It runs the official Claude Code and Codex CLIs on your computer, signed in with your own accounts. Your prompts go from your computer to Anthropic and OpenAI under your own accounts and their privacy policies. spar never sees your passwords or sign-in tokens.
- If you choose an OpenRouter model, spar sends that side's prompts to OpenRouter with your own key, and looks up OpenRouter's public model list to estimate cost. That's covered by [OpenRouter's privacy policy](https://openrouter.ai/privacy).
- No analytics, no tracking, no accounts with us. Apart from OpenRouter when you pick it, spar makes no network requests of its own.
- Match reports you save with `--save` stay in your folder.

The landing page is static, served by GitHub Pages, loads nothing from other sites, and sets no cookies. GitHub may log visits under [its privacy statement](https://docs.github.com/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-privacy-statement).

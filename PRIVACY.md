# Privacy

The spar command-line tool collects nothing.

- It runs the official Claude Code and Codex CLIs on your computer, signed in with your own accounts. Your prompts go from your computer to Anthropic and OpenAI under your own accounts and their privacy policies. spar never sees your passwords or sign-in tokens.
- No analytics, no tracking, no accounts with us. spar makes no network requests of its own.
- Match reports you save with `--save` stay in your folder.

The landing page is static, served by GitHub Pages, and sets no cookies of its own. GitHub may log visits under [its privacy statement](https://docs.github.com/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-privacy-statement).

# LinkedIn

Aim this at your own world: people writing grants, SR&ED claims, proposals and public art applications. They're the desktop app's best customers.

---

I've written a lot of funding applications this year: SR&ED claims, grant proposals, public art submissions.

The best trick I found: have Claude write a draft, then ask ChatGPT what a tough reviewer would object to. Then send the objections back. They catch different things, and they're better at catching each other's mistakes than their own.

The copy-paste got old, so I built a tool that does it for me. Two AIs, one draft, back and forth until neither can find a problem. I steer on every turn.

It's free and open source (link in comments), and it uses the Claude and ChatGPT subscriptions you already have. A desktop version with projects and your chat history is coming. Message me if you want early access.
